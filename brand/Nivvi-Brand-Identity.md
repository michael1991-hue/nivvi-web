# Nivvi brand identity

Calm caregiver product. Not clinical. Not a toy. Not a medical device.

**Name:** Nivvi  
**URL:** https://nivvi.app  
**Email:** hello.nivvi@outlook.com  
**Tagline:** Heart-rate readings on the iPhone nearby.

---

## Mark

Heart on dark teal, with a coral pulse line through it. Already used as the iPhone icon.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `logo-icon.svg` | App icon, box corner, favicon |
| `logo-wordmark.svg` | Website, letters, packaging front |
| `logo-horizontal.svg` | Header, email, letterhead |
| `strap-print.svg` | White **NIVVI** on black nylon |

Do not outline the heart in red. Do not add a baby, owl, or ECG grid.

---

## Type

- **Display:** Fraunces semibold — the word *Nivvi*
- **UI / body:** Figtree
- **Strap / embroidery:** Figtree Bold or Helvetica Bold, all caps **NIVVI**, tracking +80–120, **white** on black nylon

Never Comic Sans, never medical DIN, never neon.

---

## Voice

Short. Plain. UK English.

Yes: *Live readings on the nursery iPhone. Not a medical monitor.*  
No: *clinical-grade*, *saves lives*, *SVT detector*, *999*, *FDA*.

---

## Strap

- Print **NIVVI** once, outside of the strap, 18–24 mm cap height
- Sensor pod on the **inside** of the wrist
- Child strap 10–15 cm wrist; adult 15–22 cm
- No other logos, QR, or “OEM” marks on the wearable

---

## Packaging (sample)

Matte cream sleeve, Ink type, small icon top-left.

Front: **Nivvi**  
Line 2: *Bluetooth heart-rate band for the Nivvi iPhone app*  
Line 3: *nivvi.app*  
Footer: *Not a medical device.*

Contents: 1 sensor, 1 child strap, 1 adult strap, charger, English insert.

---

## Clear space

Keep a heart-width of empty space around the icon. Do not put the wordmark on a busy photo. Do not stretch.

---

## Legal line (always)

Nivvi is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition. Follow your clinician’s care plan.
