Access

How testers get the iPhone app

1 · TestFlight

Install Nivvi

iPhone only, iOS 16+. Email hello.nivvi@outlook.com with the Apple ID on that phone. Install TestFlight, accept the invite, then install Nivvi. Already invited? TestFlight → Nivvi → Update.

2 · Same Wi‑Fi

Downstairs phone

Nursery iPhone: Settings → Family sharing → Share from this iPhone. Downstairs iPhone: same Wi‑Fi, type the 4-digit code, Follow the nursery iPhone. Keep Nivvi open on the nursery phone. No PC needed.

3 · Away from home

Family account

When the home PC is on, create a Nivvi account (password 12+ characters). Each adult uses their own email. The PC serves family.nivvi.app. Remote push sounds are not on yet.

Live readings on the phone nearby

Nivvi connects to compatible Bluetooth heart-rate and pulse-oximeter devices and shows usable values as they arrive — not invented fillers when a packet is missing.

Thirty days of local history

Snapshots stay on the iPhone for today and the previous 29 calendar days. Touch a point on the graph, add a note, and export a CSV when you choose.

Alerts you configure

Optional high and low heart-rate alerts use your limits and a dwell time. They need a fresh supported reading, notification permission, and a phone that can actually sound.

How it works

Standard Bluetooth first. Claims second.

  • Scan and connect

    Nivvi looks for advertised Heart Rate and Pulse Oximeter services, including devices already connected to iOS. Names alone do not prove compatibility.

  • Keep a local story

    The first accepted measurement is saved, then snapshots at least 30 seconds apart. Alarms still evaluate every eligible sample.

  • Optional test alerts

    Low means strictly below; high means strictly above. The value must stay beyond the limit for 5–120 seconds. Gaps over ten seconds reset pending timing.

Please read this plainly

  • — Not a clinically validated monitor or emergency service.
  • — Bluetooth connection alone does not prove compatibility or accuracy.
  • — Silent mode, Focus, force-quit, range and battery can interrupt readings and alerts.
  • — Apple Watch, Oura and proprietary base stations are not connected in this build.
